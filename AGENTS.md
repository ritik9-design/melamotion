Keep the Mela portfolio, admin screens, and inquiries on TanStack routes. Studio sign-in is the email and password in `.env`. Do not send `ADMIN_PASSWORD` or `AUTH_SECRET` to the browser. Studio content lives in `data/studio.json`. Enquiry uploads live in `data/private-uploads` and are served only to a signed-in studio session at `/studio/files/`. Do not put enquiry files in `public/`. The URL catalog is `src/lib/site-urls.ts`.
